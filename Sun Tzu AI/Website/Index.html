    Tutorials

    Learn the systems that drive Sun Tzu AI. Start with the grading breakdown to understand how commanders are ranked.

    Understanding the Custom Grade System

    Every commander in Sun Tzu AI receives a letter grade based on their win percentage across all recorded battles. The grade is not a fixed rank; it is a dynamic combat score that changes as you win and lose.

    How It Works

    1. Total Games = Wins + Losses.
    2. Win Percentage = (Wins / Total Games) x 100.
    3. That percentage is converted to a letter grade from A to E.

    Grade Scale

    A
    B
    C
    D
    E
    • Grade A: Win rate greater than 80% — elite battlefield performance.
    • Grade B: Win rate greater than 60% — strong tactical command.
    • Grade C: Win rate greater than 40% — average effectiveness.
    • Grade D: Win rate greater than 20% — below average, room to improve.
    • Grade E: Win rate at 20% or below — requires strategic reassessment.

    Example

    A commander with 60 victories and 40 defeats has fought 100 total games. The win percentage is 60%, which earns a Grade B. The profile popup shows this as 100 60 / 40: Grade B. The larger number is always listed first.
